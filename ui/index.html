      This screen
      
  
      Left is the camera's picture: the blue line is where the eyes go, the shaded bands are where Instagram puts its own text. Right, from the top: Video or Stills, what you are making and the checks before a take, then the picture (brighter, warmer, level it, or ask in your own words), then your takes. Record is always at the bottom. In Stills you pick a look in plain words (High bokeh, Everything sharp…) instead of settings; Shoot takes one photo, shows it with its measurements and lists it under Photos. Hover anything for a short explanation (press and hold on a phone). Every dial is behind More controls.
